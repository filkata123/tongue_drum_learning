Real-drum chord check

Throwaway experiment for the wayfinder task "Record chords on the real drum and check the detector hears both tongues". It asks for 82 strikes (about 10 minutes): a few single notes, then pairs of tongues struck together. Put the laptop near the drum, in a quiet room, and press Start. Strike once per prompt, then wait for the next one. If a strike went wrong (a slip, a missed tongue), press Redo. At the end, press both Save buttons and put the two files in the recordings/ folder.
