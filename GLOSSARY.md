# Tongue Drum Practice

A practice app for one learner and one drum: a 13" 15-note D major tongue drum. The aim is to
read a note and hit the right tongue without looking at the drum.

## Language

**Tongue**:
One playable tab on the drum; each sounds one note. The drum has 15.
_Avoid_: Key, tab, pad

**Number notation**:
The way tongues are named on the drum and on song sheets: `1`–`7` for the middle row, a dot
below for the lower notes, a dot above for the upper notes.
_Avoid_: Jianpu (fine as background, but not used in the app)

**Mallet**:
One of the two sticks the drum is played with, one per hand.
_Avoid_: Stick, beater

**Song sheet**:
A written-out piece in number notation, as found printed or online for tongue drums.
_Avoid_: Score, tab

**Two-line sheet**:
A song sheet with a melody line on top and an accompaniment line below, bracketed together and
played at the same time. Numbers stacked in the same column are struck together.
_Avoid_: Double sheet, two-hand sheet

**Melody line**:
The top line of a two-line sheet; the tune.

**Accompaniment line**:
The bottom line of a two-line sheet; mostly lower notes that support the tune.
_Avoid_: Bass line, harmony line, left-hand line

**Chord**:
Two tongues struck at the same moment. Strictly a "dyad" (a chord is three or more notes), but
"2-note chord" is the usual term in tongue drum material.
_Avoid_: Double stop, dyad, pair

**Mode**:
A way of practising in the app, with its own rules for when a note counts and when to move on.

**Step mode**:
The mode that shows one note, waits as long as needed, and moves on when the right tongue is hit.
_Avoid_: Free mode, practice mode

**Timed mode**:
A mode where notes must be hit in time with a beat. The beat keeps going whether or not you hit;
a missed note is marked and the sheet moves on.
_Avoid_: Rhythm mode

**Listen**:
The app plays the part you're practising, with each number lit as it sounds. Only ever on
request; it never plays by itself.
_Avoid_: Preview, demo, playback

**Drum map**:
A picture of the 15 tongues laid out as they sit on the drum in front of you.
_Avoid_: Diagram, keyboard

**Location hint**:
A drum map with the target tongue lit, shown only when you're stuck on a step. It fades for each
tongue separately as you find that tongue without it.
_Avoid_: Highlight, help, cue

**Song text**:
A song sheet typed out in the app's plain-text form: a title, a melody line and an optional
accompaniment line, with note lengths written as keyboard marks. It's what you paste into the app.
_Avoid_: Song file, tab, transcription

**Conversion prompt**:
The rules for writing song text, copied out of the app so a chatbot can turn a song sheet image
into song text.
_Avoid_: Ruleset, export rules

**My songs**:
The songs you've pasted into the app, saved on that device only.
_Avoid_: Library, custom songs

**Piece**:
Anything you can practise in the app: an exercise or a song from My songs. Both play the same way.
_Avoid_: Melody, track, tune

**Exercise**:
A short built-in piece for practising, not a real song. It can't be edited.
_Avoid_: Melody, drill, lesson

**Made-up tune**:
An exercise the app makes up fresh: a short tune in an order you can't predict, shaped to sound
like music (small moves, ending on 1). Practising it means reading every note, because there's
nothing to remember.
_Avoid_: Random drill, random melody, shuffle

**First-strike hit**:
A step played right on the first strike: the right tongue (or both tongues of a chord) with no
Location hint shown. In Timed mode, hit inside the beat window.
_Avoid_: Correct note, clean hit

**Cold run**:
The first full play-through of a piece (in one part) on a given day. Its score, first-strike hits
out of steps, is how progress is measured. Every made-up tune is a cold run.
_Avoid_: First try, daily score, attempt
